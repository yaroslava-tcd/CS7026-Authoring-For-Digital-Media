My Projects

Here are some of the projects I've worked on:

Project 1: Kyiv City Magazine

This magazine's layout was created using InDesign. Vector graphics and photo editing were done with Adobe Illustrator and Adobe Photoshop.

Project 2: Furry Friends Website

This is a pet services website I designed and developed. The HTML, CSS, and JavaScript libraries such as Bootstrap were used to create the website from scratch. Figma was used for wireframes and mock-ups.

Project 3: Maynooth Library Cat Documentary

A short documentary that was later posted on the official social media account of Maynooth University. Programs used: Audition and Premiere Pro. Our team worked with different professional equipment such as Boom Mic, Tripod, Zoom H1 and Canon Camera.

Project 4: Kinetica

This is my university final year project where I designed a brand identity for an app that helps young peope connect and explore their creativity. It was later implemented as a functional prototype.
